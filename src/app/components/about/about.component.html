About Me

Software from interface to infrastructure

I’m a software engineer with hands-on backend experience in Java, Spring Boot, and microservices, alongside frontend development and AI research. My interests span the full software lifecycle: building interfaces, designing APIs, automating delivery, and preparing applications for the cloud.

My backend work includes REST APIs, JWT and OAuth 2.0 authentication, MySQL persistence, and event-driven communication with Apache Kafka and RabbitMQ. I also build web interfaces with Angular and React, and explore Docker, Jenkins, and Kubernetes through DevOps projects and practice.

At ScleraVDMS, I worked on reusable report templates, inspection job queues, offline reminders, and versioned data models. I also contributed to the design of a transition from a monolithic backend to domain-focused microservices.

As project lead and research presenter, I coordinated an AI-based system for converting 2D blueprints into 3D models. Our work was presented at IEEE AIDE 2026 and published in IEEE Xplore.
